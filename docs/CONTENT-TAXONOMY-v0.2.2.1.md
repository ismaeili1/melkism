# MELKISM v0.2.2.1
# Content Types & Taxonomy

## Purpose

This release establishes the canonical content architecture
for MELKISM.

The architecture is independent from MELKIST.

## Content Types

- News
- Articles
- Research
- Reports
- Markets
- Trends
- Data
- Cities
- Countries

## Content Layers

### Editorial

Human-readable and publication-oriented content.

### Research

Research-oriented material, studies and academic knowledge.

### Intelligence

Interpretation, analysis, market intelligence and strategic insight.

### Data

Structured information intended for analysis, comparison and
future data products.

## Lifecycle

Content status:

1. Draft
2. Review
3. Verified
4. Scheduled
5. Published
6. Archived

## Taxonomy Dimensions

- Topic
- Country
- City

## Architectural Principle

Taxonomy belongs to MELKISM.

It must not depend on MELKIST.

Future integrations must consume MELKISM contracts
through an explicit integration boundary.

## Future Extensions

Later releases may introduce:

- Source provenance
- Content entities
- multilingual content
- editorial workflow
- ingestion pipelines
- RSS/API connectors
- automated translation
- AI-assisted classification
- search indexing
- knowledge graph relationships

These are intentionally outside v0.2.2.1.
